Put real photographs here if you would rather reference files than upload them
through the CMS.

Suggested layout:
  assets/portfolio/<project-slug>/01.webp
  assets/services/<service-slug>/cover.webp
  assets/about/portrait.webp

Export at 1600 px on the long edge for display copies and 420 px for
thumbnails. WebP at quality 75 is a good default for photography.
